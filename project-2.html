ABOUT

Alfred is a LATAM fintech built for fast, simple money movement. We designed the brand, the website, and the app, and built the site. The platform now moves 12K transactions a month.

CHALLENGE

Alfred was raising money and partnering with banks while still looking like a prototype. It needed a brand and product design that could win over regulators, banks, and everyday users all at once.

SOLUTION

A clean wordmark with a directional arrow, a dark and confident visual system, and web and app interfaces built for trust and speed. It helped back a $1M investment from Stellar and more than 5 bank partnerships across LATAM.
